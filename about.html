Why Handcrafted Wood?

Modern electric appliances often break down and use toxic non-stick coatings. Traditional rolling pins demand heavy physical wrist strain.

Our manual wooden roti presses use natural mechanical leverage, solid seasoned wood, and food-safe smooth surfaces to press perfectly round, thin chapatis, jowar rotis, and holige in under 2 seconds.
