Care & Usage Best Practices

  • Use Oil / Plastic Sheet: For best release and perfectly round shape, place dough between two lightly greased food-grade sheets or butter paper.
  • Gentle Pressure: The natural weight of the wooden top plate plus light downward leverage gives instant flattening without high effort.
  • Cleaning: Wipe clean with a dry or slightly damp cloth after each use. Do not submerge or soak in water.
  • Wood Conditioning: Wipe lightly with edible vegetable oil once a month to preserve the rich natural wooden luster and moisture resistance.
  • Storage: Store upright or flat in a dry, ventilated kitchen shelf.
