Quick & Easy

From Dough to Perfection in 4 Simple Steps

Anyone in the family can make round, soft rotis in seconds without prior rolling experience.

1

Prepare Dough

Make a smooth dough ball and lightly apply oil on both sides or use a clean food-grade plastic/butter sheet.

2

Place in Press

Center the dough portion between the pressing surfaces of the wooden base plate.

3

Press with Handle

Gently bring down the handle and apply light downward pressure for 2 seconds.

4

Remove & Cook

Lift the top plate, peel the evenly flattened roti onto a hot tawa, and cook until fluffy and golden.
