Why Upgrade?

Traditional Rolling Pin vs. Shrimati Roti Press

See why thousands of home cooks are making the smart switch.

Feature Traditional Rolling Pin (Belan) Shrimati Wooden Roti Press
Preparation Time 2 to 3 minutes per roti with continuous rolling ⚡ Under 2 seconds per press
Effort & Strain Heavy wrist and shoulder pressure required ✋ Gentle, smooth ergonomic downward leverage
Shape & Uniformity Often uneven, requires rolling skill ⭕ Perfectly uniform circle every single time
Jowar & Millet Rotis Difficult to pat without dough tearing 🌾 Flattens delicate millet dough smoothly
Holige / Obbattu High risk of sweet stuffing spilling out 🍯 Even spread with zero stuffing leakage
Simple & Fast

How It Works in 4 Steps

1

Prepare Dough

Knead a smooth dough and portion a ball. Place between lightly oiled sheets.

2

Place in Press

Center the dough portion on the wooden base plate.

3

Press Lever

Gently bring down the handle for 2 seconds to flatten.

4

Transfer & Cook

Peel onto the tawa and cook until soft and puffed.
